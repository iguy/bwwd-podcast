Why does focus show up under stress but feel out of reach on an ordinary day? In this episode of *Better World With Design*, I review Anna Lembke’s *Dopamine Nation: Finding Balance in the Age of Indulgence*. I walk through her pleasure-pain balance, the opponent-process and incentive-sensitization theories behind it, the gambling study she cites, dopamine fasting, self-binding, radical honesty, and prosocial shame. I weigh what her patient stories and evidence can and can’t prove, where the line between a habit and an addiction matters, and why I wanted more depth on stress, attention, and AuDHD.

*This episode is personal reflection, not medical advice.*
